Teacher: one-time setup
  1. Upload arduino/eye_servo/eye_servo.ino to the Arduino Uno/Nano once, using the Arduino IDE.
  2. Wire the servos: side servo signal → pin D9, up/down servo signal → pin D10, both red wires → external 5 V supply (+), both brown wires → supply GND and Arduino GND.
  3. Students then only plug in the USB cable and use this page. They never need the Arduino IDE.
Something not working?
  • No Arduino in the list: try another USB cable (some only charge) or another port. Clone boards may need the CH340 driver.
  • “Failed to open serial port”: close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor and any other program using the Arduino.
  • Servo moves the wrong way: tick or untick “Reverse side direction” or “Reverse up/down direction”.
  • One-eye mode does nothing: check the “Eyes:” line under the dials. The closed eye should say “closed”. If your open eye also says “closed”, open it wider or add light.
  • Up/down does not move: upload the new eye_servo.ino (it reads “side,up/down”) and press Calibrate again.
  • Servo twitches or the Arduino keeps disconnecting: power the servo from a separate battery pack or supply, with the grounds joined.
  • “No face found”: face the camera, add more light, and remove glare from glasses if you can.
